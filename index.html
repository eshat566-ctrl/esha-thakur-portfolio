What I actually spend my time doing

I'm currently reading for an MBA in Business Analytics at Lovely Professional University, after finishing a B.Com in Finance at DAV University, Jalandhar. The pull toward analytics started in finance — once you've built a ratio analysis by hand, you start wanting the tools to do it faster and better.

Most of what I do sits at the join between numbers and a decision: researching a market before a pitch, working out whether a cost target is realistic, or reading a company's five years of filings to say something useful about where it's headed. I keep coming back to Excel, Power BI and Python for the heavy lifting, and to plain writing for the part where someone else has to act on it.
